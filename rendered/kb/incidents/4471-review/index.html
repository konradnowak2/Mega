Problems and independent fixes

  • Shared parser: day-first tenant input is interpreted month first, swapping dates such as 2 December and 12 February. Respect the tenant’s date convention or normalize explicit structured dates before parsing. Check app, email and booking-v2 paths, plus app/email from booking-v3 tenants.
  • booking-v3 hint: the form prints MM/DD/YYYY for day-first tenants while the form reads input in the tenant’s order. Align the hint and actual parsing. A shared-parser fix alone does not correct this misleading form. Check alder.physio (de-DE), copper.studio (es-ES) and pale.harbour (pt-PT) on the form channel.

Examples: R-41203 is a parser report from lantern.row; R-41230 is a booking-v3 form report from copper.studio even though intake tagged it parser. R-41205 is a parser report from morrow.tax despite its en-US browser locale and hint tag. The tenant directory, not the reporter’s browser, controls the classification.
