0002 · Merge reports by established cause

owner Konrad Nowak and OpenCode proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Context

The review of #4471 demonstrates that subject matching collapsed independent parser and booking-form-hint defects into one ticket. The old decision 0001 remains intact as an accepted historical record.

Replacement rule

This proposal supersedes subject-only matching for the reviewed incident. Establish the tenant, its locale, form version and report channel. Merge only reports sharing a verified cause. Subject similarity is a search aid, not a merge decision. When evidence is missing or symptoms conflict with the classifier, retain the report for investigation.

For this export, causeOf and sameCause implement the operational distinction. Their output is a classification rather than proof of a production root cause; anomalous report bodies still need investigation.
